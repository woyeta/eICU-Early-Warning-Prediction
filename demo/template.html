    Spotting ICU deterioration early

    An early-warning system that reads a patient’s hourly vital signs and lab results and estimates the chance they will need blood-pressure drugs, a breathing machine, or die in the next 24 hours, so staff can step in sooner.

    The problem

    Bedside scores like NEWS2 look at a single moment. They miss slow trends and raise many false alarms, which leads to alarm fatigue.

    The idea

    Give models the patient’s recent history (the last 6 to 48 hours), not just the current hour, and compare them fairly with NEWS2.

    The data

    eICU-CRD Demo v2.0.1: real, de-identified ICU stays from 186 US hospitals, with hourly vitals, labs, drugs and outcomes.
